## Launch writing-craft boundary

Writing Craft & Invisible Comprehension v0.1 is a **launch-format editorial policy**, not a new system layer and not a universal product doctrine.

For Build 2:

- Craft Critic runs inside `SCRIPTED` after Pass 1 and before speech texture;
- the Critic diagnoses and never rewrites or adds sports facts;
- zero or one substantive craft revision is allowed;
- comprehension targets are claim-backed programming metadata, not repetition quotas;
- no `repeat_count`, `desired_mentions`, `desired_reinforcement`, facts-per-minute, or insights-per-minute optimization is permitted;
- insight/implication assertions cannot outrun frozen claims;
- a dedicated craft-review table/service is not required and must not be added without a demonstrated gap in the existing immutable artifact/model-run mechanism;
- production-scale Craft Library retrieval, attention-loss simulation, and audience-metric-driven policy rewriting are deferred.

The launch objective is to make valuable, supportable insight easy to retain under imperfect attention while the listener experiences entertainment, not instruction.

## Character/ensemble boundary

Character Bible v0.1.4 strengthens personality without weakening the Antarctica rule:

- the covered club is the shared object of concern, but no synthetic participant claims supporter membership, attendance, personal history, or off-air biography;
- Simon is an emotionally expressive supporter avatar, not a detached sentiment reporter and not an argument machine;
- Gaz is a listening/processing thinker with dry intellectual humor, not a serial stat dispenser;
- Gaz and Simon are warm complementary mates whose disagreements are earned;
- Tully owns the closing synthesis as an editorial landing, not a fixed recap template.

## Legacy hazard rule

Never implement from unbannered copies of Lore Pipeline v0.1 or Ingest & Digest v0.1. Re-derive pronunciation, retention, incident/sombre, and rights behavior from the active successor chain.
